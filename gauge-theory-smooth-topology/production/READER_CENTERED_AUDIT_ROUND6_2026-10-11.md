# Reader-centered audit — Round 6

## Reader model

The audit uses the Round 6 reader model: linear algebra, calculus, and elementary ODE/PDE are allowed; topology, manifolds, differential forms, bundles, gauge theory, Sobolev spaces, spinors, complex geometry, knot theory, and symplectic geometry are not assumed.

## High-risk stop points and actions

| Location | Reader stop point | Fix made | Acceptance check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chapter 2 before differential forms | "What is a tangent vector if it has not been introduced yet?" | Added tangent/cotangent construction before differential forms. | A reader can now parse a 1-form as a linear functional on $T_pX$. |
| Chapter 2 self-intersection | "How can a surface intersect itself if it is the same set?" | Expanded push-off by a normal-bundle section and related zeros to the Euler number. | A reader can distinguish transverse intersection, push-off, and zero of a section. |
| Chapter 8/9 principal symbol | "Why are we computing this symbol sequence before learning what a symbol is?" | Moved Fourier/high-frequency motivation into Chapter 8 before symbol-sequence calculation. | Chapter 9 no longer supplies the first real explanation after the fact. |
| Chapter 12 $\mu$ map | "What does slant product do to degrees?" | Added the type calculation $p_1(\mathbb P)/\alpha\in H^{4-i}(\mathcal B^*)$. | Degree matching is now derived from the product-space class. |
| Chapter 13 Donaldson outputs | "Is this a real calculation or only a model?" | Reworded the construction section to identify it as a construction model and named the $E_8$ exclusion as the concrete Donaldson output. | The text no longer presents a model as a computed Donaldson polynomial. |
| Chapter 14 knot surgery | "What are meridian, complement, and Alexander polynomial?" | Added a minimal knot-theory vocabulary section before knot surgery. | The Fintushel--Stern formula has defined inputs. |
| Chapter 15 half-spinors | "Why do $W^+$ and $W^-$ appear?" | Added $\mathrm{Spin}(4)\cong SU(2)_+\times SU(2)_-$ and the role of Clifford action between halves. | The two half-spinor bundles are motivated before use. |

## Result

Round 6 does not claim that the book has become a full replacement for courses in topology, differential geometry, analysis, and gauge theory. It does claim that the audited high-impact first-use reversals were corrected in the editable source, and that the remaining research-level inputs are marked as cited results rather than silently used elementary facts.
