# Math Conventions Round 3

## Freedman Classification

For closed, simply connected, oriented topological 4-manifolds, the intersection form and Kirby--Siebenmann data must be handled with parity:

- even unimodular form: the Kirby--Siebenmann invariant is constrained by the signature, and the oriented homeomorphism type is unique in the version used here;
- odd unimodular form: two homeomorphism types may occur for the same form, distinguished by the Kirby--Siebenmann invariant.

This is now used consistently in Chapters 3 and 14.

## Fredholm Argument

Kernel finite-dimensionality is proved using an L2-orthonormal sequence in the kernel, not a Sobolev-norm-normalized sequence. The elliptic estimate gives uniform Sobolev boundedness, and Rellich compactness contradicts L2 orthonormal separation.

## Spin-c and SW Type Convention

The textbook keeps the determinant-line connection convention:

$$
(A,\Phi)\mapsto(A-2u^{-1}du,u\Phi).
$$

The quadratic map is read as an $i\Lambda^2_+$-valued form through the Clifford identification with trace-free Hermitian endomorphisms.

## Invariance

Gauge-theoretic counts are diffeomorphism invariants after regularity, orientation, compactness, and cobordism. They are not declared non-topological merely because their definition is smooth; non-topological behavior is justified by examples where homeomorphic smooth manifolds have different gauge invariants.
