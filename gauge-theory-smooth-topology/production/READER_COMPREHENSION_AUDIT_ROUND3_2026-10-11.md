# Reader Comprehension Audit Round 3

## Reader Risk Points Addressed

- Homology and intersection form: Chapter 2 now distinguishes smooth realization from topological realization before invoking Freedman.
- Freedman vs Donaldson: Chapter 3 presents the parity and Kirby--Siebenmann logic before using E8.
- Fredholm theory: Chapter 9 expands the finite-dimensionality argument enough for a reader with basic PDE background to follow the contradiction.
- Spin-c structures: Chapter 15 replaces the misleading torsor translation and states the action as a free transitive group action after choosing a base structure.
- Donaldson/SW/topology bridge: Chapter 18 no longer asserts non-topological invariance from smooth definition alone.

## Residual Scope Note

The revision addresses the concrete round 3 blockers and language failures. It does not claim to have expanded the book to a full 300-page undergraduate-to-research bridge; it preserves the current compact textbook architecture while removing the identified acceptance blockers.
